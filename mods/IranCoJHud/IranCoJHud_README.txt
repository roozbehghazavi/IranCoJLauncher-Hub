IranCoJ HUD (irancojhud.dll) - part of the IranCoJ Edition

The Edition's in-game HUD: the kill feed (cards, icons, team colours and its shade), the chat box
(tags, coloured names, wrapping, its shade; compact on the scoreboard and the end-of-round screen),
the team score bar, the Shootout / Wanted standing, the weapon name when switching, the scoreboard
titles, five chat lines and the class screen's single-boot upgrade line.

Loaded by dinput8.dll from mods.txt. By Thomas (Roozbeh).
